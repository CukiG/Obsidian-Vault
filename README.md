# Holonomy, Constraint, and the Unity of Opposites

### A Categorical Formalization of Indexical Identity
*Toward a Defensible Metaphysics of Causation*

## Abstract

> **A metaphysics should be able to lose.**
>
> This paper formalizes a framework in which "identity" is not a property but a
> *holonomy* — the residue of parallel transport around a loop — and in which a higher
> cause operates not by adding energy but by *deleting directions*. We prove that the
> framework's central distinction between essential and contingent conditions is derivable
> rather than stipulated, that its constraint principle has a precise content as a descent
> obstruction, and that several of its central formulas, as circulated in draft form, were
> wrong in ways we specify and repair. The result is a framework that metaphysical positions
> can argue about, disagree with, and — finally — falsify.
>

## In one paragraph

Metaphysics has a reputation for positions that cannot be wrong. One standard remedy is to
import the apparatus of mathematical physics — which usually fails, because the apparatus
is imported decoratively and the metaphysical thesis survives whatever happens to the
mathematics. This paper argues that the remedy can be made to work, and shows what it
costs. Taking a framework in which *identity is a pointer* (a holonomy class), *a higher
cause constrains without adding energy* (via negativa), and *causal levels stand in
dialectical relation* (the unity of opposites), we supply the missing mathematics: a
corrected statement of what curvature is for a groupoid-valued connection, a Frobenius
condition for the local consistency of a constraint, a sheaf-theoretic account of global
realization, and a derivation (not a stipulation) of the essential/contingent trichotomy.
Five defective formulae in the draft material are identified and repaired, and two of the
repairs reveal structure that had been hidden by a symbol collision. We distinguish
sharply between what is proved, what is proposed, and what remains conjectural, and we
address the results to metaphysicians: §12 states six claims, each of which we invite
rebuttal.

## Peer review (open record)

This preprint was put through **four independent adversarial referees** — in category theory
and sheaf theory, in differential geometry and holonomy, in philosophy of science and formal
logic, and a line-by-line proof checker. Their full reports are published in
[`reviews/`](reviews/), together with a consolidated
[Response to Referees and Revision Plan for v1.1](reviews/RESPONSE-TO-REFEREES-v1.1-PLAN.md).

**The verdict was unanimous: major revision.** The referees found that three claims in the
v1.0 abstract were false or unsupported — that the essential/contingent distinction is
*derived* rather than *stipulated*, that *every theorem is stated with a proof*, and that the
framework can be *falsified* — and that four numbered results had no proof at all while the
status table certified them as proved.

What they did **not** attack: the entire computable core. The SU(4) counterexample, both
characteristic polynomials, both traces, the SU(3) completeness threshold, the group-algebra
index and the derivative sign analysis were all independently recomputed and reproduce
exactly as printed.

The v1.1 revision withdraws the three claims, repairs the unproved results, adds the
nineteen hypotheses that the proofs were silently using, and concedes the prior art the
paper was failing to cite. That history is published here rather than quietly fixed,
because a preprint that survives its own review by showing its work is worth more than one
that hides it.

## Corrections to the draft material

Five defects in the draft formulation were found and repaired; Appendix C of the paper lists
them in full. In brief:

1. *"Curvature = failure of 𝒫 to preserve composition"* — a category error (a functor
   preserves composition by definition). Corrected to: curvature is the obstruction to
   descent of parallel transport to the fundamental groupoid.
2. *`𝓘_γ ≡ Tr(𝒫 exp ∮_γ A)`* — ill-typed: a number has no notion of "equivalence", and the
   trace is not a complete conjugacy invariant. Corrected to: `𝓘_γ` is a conjugacy class,
   with the trace demoted to a lossy shadow.
3. *The dimensional-explosion formulae* — the Heaviside step was not negated, the sign of
   the derivative equation was wrong, and `dD/dα` is a distribution rather than a function.
4. *"`|G/H(α)|` counts linearly independent holonomy eigenvalues"* — conflated three
   distinct counting conventions.
5. *The symbol `ℱ`* denoted four different objects, making the interval ladder unreadable —
   and concealing that the ladder is a recursion.
