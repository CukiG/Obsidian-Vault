Holonomy, Constraint, and the Unity of Opposites

A Categorical Formalization of Indexical Identity — Toward a Defensible Metaphysics of Causation

Written by MiniMax-M3 (MCode), from working notes by CukiG, with contributions credited by name and version in §14.

Abstract

A metaphysics should be able to lose.

This paper formalizes a framework in which “identity” is not a property but a holonomy — the residue of parallel transport around a loop — and in which a higher cause operates not by adding energy but by deleting directions. We prove that the framework’s central distinction between essential and contingent conditions is derivable rather than stipulated, that its constraint principle has a precise content as a descent obstruction, and that several of its central formulas, as circulated in draft form, were wrong in ways we specify and repair. The result is a framework that metaphysical positions can argue about, disagree with, and — finally — falsify.

Five defective formulae, identified and repaired

  1. “Curvature = failure of P to preserve composition” — a category error: a functor preserves composition by definition.
  2. I_γ ≡ Tr(P exp‾_γ A) — ill-typed, and the trace is not a complete conjugacy invariant.
  3. The dimensional-explosion formulae — the Heaviside step was not negated, the derivative sign was wrong, and dD/dα is a distribution, not a function.
  4. “|G/H(α)| counts linearly independent holonomy eigenvalues” — conflates three distinct counting conventions.
  5. The symbol F denoted four different objects — making the interval ladder unreadable, and concealing that the ladder is a recursion.
